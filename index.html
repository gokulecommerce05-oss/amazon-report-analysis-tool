Step 1 · Add your payment files

Add one or many, from any platform. Not sure where a file is? Follow the steps on its card.

AmazonXML / CSV

Payments → All Statements → Download XML for each statement of the month

Step-by-step
  1. 1
    Open Seller Central and click Payments (top menu).
  2. 2
    Open the All Statements tab: one row per payment period (about a week).
  3. 3
    Click Download XML on every row of the month (4–5 files). If a row says Request Report, click it and come back in a few minutes.
  4. 4
    No XML? Use Transaction View → Custom date range (e.g. Oct 1 – Oct 31) → Download CSV.

FlipkartXLSX

Payments → Download Invoice / Reports → Settled Transactions report for the month

Step-by-step
  1. 1
    Open Seller Hub and click Payments in the left menu.
  2. 2
    Click Download Invoice / Reports (top right). This opens the Reports page.
  3. 3
    Click Request New Report, then choose Payment Reports.
  4. 4
    Under Settled Transactions, click Request Report.
  5. 5
    Keep One Time Request selected and set the date range, e.g. Sep 01 – Sep 30.
  6. 6
    Click Submit. You will see “Report is requested successfully”.
  7. 7
    Click the Requested box and filter by Payment. Your request shows as In Progress.
  8. 8
    Wait 1–3 minutes until it says Generated. Click another filter and back on Payment to refresh.
  9. 9
    Click Download on the row “Settled Transactions, Sep 1 To Sep 30”.

Returns reportsCSV

Flipkart: Orders → Returns → All Returns → Request Download · Meesho: Return/RTO Orders → Return Tracking → Export Data (In transit, Delivered, Lost)

Flipkart: step-by-step
  1. 1
    In Seller Hub, click Orders in the left menu, then Returns.
  2. 2
    Open the All Returns tab.
  3. 3
    Click the Returns approved in filter and pick a range. Last 90 days is the widest it allows.
  4. 4
    Click Request Download.
  5. 5
    Click Previous Downloads. When it says Ready to download (a few seconds), click Download. You get a CSV of every RTO and customer return, with order IDs.
Meesho: step-by-step
  1. 1
    In the Supplier Panel, open the ☰ menu → Orders → Return/RTO Orders.
  2. 2
    Click the Return Tracking tab (next to Overview).
  3. 3
    Pick the status tab: In transit, Delivered or Lost (the tool uses these three).
  4. 4
    Click the Return Created filter and choose Custom date range, e.g. 01/10/2026 – 31/10/2026.
  5. 5
    On the right, click the “0/0 files ready ⌄” box.
  6. 6
    Click the purple Export Data button. It takes a few minutes.
  7. 7
    Under Exported Files, click ⬇ Download to save the file.
  8. 8
    Repeat steps 3–7 for each tab: 3 files in total.

Returns & RTO from your returns reports

Every return in the files you added, matched to your Orders tab for COD, courier and state. Orders shipped in the last 7–10 days may still come back, so recent rates look lower.

RTO = the courier brought the parcel back without delivering it. Customer return = delivered, then sent back. "Check or claim" lists parcels lost in transit, returns still not back after 30 days, and customer returns for damaged, missing or wrong items: open those on video when they arrive and file a claim if the item is damaged, empty or different.

Sorted by money lost

    Start at the top: HIGH first, then MEDIUM. Each item says what to do.

    Unit economics & pricing

    Profit per order for each product, the price where it breaks even, and the price that reaches your target margin. Click a column name to sort; type a price in "Try price" to see the new profit. The header row and product column stay frozen while you scroll.

    Fixed cost per order = platform fees, shipping and product cost, which stay about the same if you change the price. "Lost per ₹100" = returns, discounts, TCS/TDS and the GST you owe, which grow with the price. Break-even and target prices assume those stay the same, and sales volume is unchanged when you try a new price. Profit here subtracts GST on sales, so it is lower than "Profit after product cost" elsewhere.

    States

    Uses the Orders tab of your business sheet (filled by the label tool) to find each order's payment type, courier and state, matched by order ID. "Charges lost" = shipping, return shipping and platform fees on orders that came back; your own packaging and handling are extra. A state is flagged when it has at least 5 orders and comes back much more often than your average.

    Orders by price band

    Under each column: how much of every ₹100 reached your bank

    The tallest column is the price most customers buy at. If cheap orders keep much less of every ₹100, shipping and fees are eating them: raise those prices or sell combos.

    Refunds by payment type

    Free orders

    Refunds

    Show every order
    Free order means the promotional rebate equalled the full product price. Open one in Manage Orders: if it is labelled Vine it is a Vine unit; otherwise check Advertising → Promotions for a 100%-off promotion or promo code. Deferred amounts are held by Amazon until after delivery and are paid in later settlements.

    Money to recover

    Money the platforms may owe you: orders never paid, shipping charged above what the same product usually costs, and returns to claim. Uses every file you added, all dates.

    "Usual" shipping = the middle charge for single-unit orders of the same product to the same state (needs at least 4 such orders, so state and distance do not cause false alarms). Add a "Packed weight (g)" column to the Purchase Cost tab to compare it with the weight Flipkart measures.

    Profit & loss

    Month by month, by payment date: what you sold, what the platforms kept, and what is left after product cost, packing and your fixed costs.

    Fixed monthly costs rent, salary, internet, software

    Saved only in this browser. Fixed costs are split by the days of each month your files cover. Product cost counts only units the buyer kept (RTO and returns go back to stock). TCS/TDS is shown as a cost here because it was not paid to you, but you get it back when you file.

    AI assistant

    Get a key at console.anthropic.com → API Keys. It is saved only in this browser. When you type your own question, a summary of your report (totals, months, products, fees, returns by state and courier; no customer names or addresses) is sent to Anthropic's Claude to answer it, about ₹1–4 per question on your Anthropic bill.
